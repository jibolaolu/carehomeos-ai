# Clinical Safety

CareHomeOS separates AI assistance from accountable clinical decision-making. AI outputs are routed through quality gates, safeguarding keyword checks, senior review queues, and immutable audit logs before they become part of the resident record.

High-risk workflows include eMAR omissions, falls, deterioration, safeguarding disclosures, and pressure care concerns. Each workflow records the source note, routing decision, reviewer, timestamp, and CQC quality statement tags.
